### Scenarios (3):

- baseline = realtime only (no encryption, no translation)
- e2ee = encryption on, translation off
- e2ee_translate = encryption on, translation on

### Message sizes (3):

- small = ~20 chars
- medium = ~200 chars
- large = ~2000 chars

### Runs: 10 runs per (scenario × size)

- Total files: 3 × 3 × 10 = 90 exports
